EventBetter

Recurring events where every occurrence has its own title

In Google Calendar, every occurrence of a recurring event shares one title. EventBetter, an Android app, creates recurring events where each occurrence is numbered, so you can see at a glance how far along you are.

Oct 30, 2026 Tuff Gym membership: 1st month over
Nov 30, 2026 Tuff Gym membership: 2nd month over
Dec 30, 2026 Tuff Gym membership: 3rd month over

How it works

  • One real recurring event. EventBetter creates a single recurring series in Google Calendar, so deleting it there offers “All events” like any other series.
  • Titles you choose. Use a template such as {name}: {ord} {unit} over or {name}: {elapsed} {units} done, and every occurrence gets its own number.
  • Any rhythm. Repeat every few days, weeks, months or years, and stop after a set number of occurrences or on an end date. Month ends are handled correctly: a series starting on the 31st lands on the last day of shorter months.
  • Reminders. A Google Calendar reminder and an optional phone notification at 9:00 AM the day before each occurrence.
  • Edit or delete anytime. Change a recurring event and EventBetter rebuilds it, or delete it with all of its occurrences.

Your Calendar, kept separate

EventBetter signs in with your Google account and asks for Calendar access. It creates its own calendar named “EventBetter” and only creates and changes events in that calendar, never in your other calendars. It also reads the names of your calendars, so it can find its own calendar again if you reinstall the app or switch phones.

There is no EventBetter server: your data goes only between your phone and Google. Your Google data is never used for advertising. The details are in the privacy policy.

Free, with one ad

EventBetter is free and shows one banner ad on its main screen. It's also open source: the code is on GitHub under the MIT license.
