EventBetter

Privacy policy

Effective October 5, 2026

This policy explains what the EventBetter Android app (“EventBetter”, “the app”) does with your information. EventBetter is published by jumppack. If you have questions, email support@jumppack.online.

In short

  • EventBetter has no server. Your calendar data goes only between your phone and Google.
  • It only creates and changes events in its own “EventBetter” calendar, never in your other calendars.
  • Your Google user data is never used for advertising and is never shared with the ad network.
  • The app shows ads through Google AdMob, which collects some device data as described below.
  • You can disconnect and delete everything at any time.

Google data the app accesses, and why

EventBetter asks for access in two steps, and only uses each permission for the purpose listed.

When you sign in with Google

  • Your name, email address and profile picture. Used only to show which account you're signed in with and to request Calendar access for that account. They're stored only on your phone.

When you allow Calendar access

  • Create a secondary calendar and manage events on it (calendar.app.created). EventBetter creates one calendar named “EventBetter” and creates, reads, changes and deletes the recurring events you make in the app, only in that calendar. Each recurring event stores the settings you entered (title, dates, repeat rule and title templates) in Google Calendar itself, so you can edit it later.
  • See the list of calendars you're subscribed to (calendar.calendarlist.readonly). EventBetter reads your calendars' names and descriptions only to find its own calendar again after you reinstall the app or switch phones. It doesn't read events from your other calendars and doesn't store this list.

You can untick the calendar-list permission on Google's consent screen; the app still works, but can't find its calendar again after a reinstall.

Where your data is stored

  • In your Google Calendar: the EventBetter calendar and the recurring events you create, including the settings described above.
  • On your phone: your account name and email, the ID of the EventBetter calendar, and which reminder notifications are scheduled. Uninstalling the app removes this.
  • Access tokens are managed by Google Play services on your phone. EventBetter doesn't store them.

EventBetter has no server, no database of its own and no analytics service. Nothing about you or your calendars is sent to the developer. All communication with Google uses encrypted HTTPS connections.

Reminders

Each recurring event gets a Google Calendar reminder. If you allow notifications, the app also schedules local notifications on your phone for the next occurrences. These are created on the device and are not sent through any server.

Advertising

EventBetter is free and shows one banner ad from Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects:

  • device identifiers, such as your phone's advertising ID and app set ID
  • your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location
  • information about your device and the app, such as model, operating system and diagnostics
  • interactions with ads, such as views and taps

Google processes this data under its own policies; see how Google uses information from apps that use its services. If you're in the European Economic Area, the UK or a US state with privacy laws, the app asks for your consent choices before any ad loads, and you can change them anytime in Settings → Ads → Manage ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

The app never gives the ad network any Google user data: no calendar data, event titles, account name or email address. Ads aren't targeted using anything from your Google account or calendars. The ad code is kept separate from the calendar and account code, and checks in the source code enforce this.

Google API Services User Data Policy

EventBetter's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

In particular, Google user data obtained through EventBetter is:

  • used only to provide and improve the app's user-facing features described above
  • not transferred to anyone, except as needed to provide those features, to comply with the law, or as part of a merger or acquisition with notice to you
  • never used or transferred for advertising, including personalized, retargeted or interest-based ads
  • never sold, and never used to determine creditworthiness or for lending
  • not read by any person, unless you give explicit permission for a specific case (for example, to help with a support request), it's needed for security or abuse investigation, or required by law

Revoking access and deleting your data

  • Delete your recurring events: in the app, delete one from its card, or use Settings → Danger zone → Delete all recurring events. You can also delete the “EventBetter” calendar in Google Calendar.
  • Revoke access: in the app, use Settings → Danger zone → Disconnect Google account, which removes the app's access and clears its data on your phone. You can also remove EventBetter from your Google Account's third-party connections at any time.
  • Delete the app's data on your phone: sign out, or uninstall the app.

Because EventBetter has no server, the developer holds no copy of your data to delete. Data in your Google Calendar stays until you delete it.

Children

EventBetter isn't directed at children under 13, and the developer doesn't knowingly collect their information.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If a change affects how Google user data is used, the app will ask for your consent before the change applies to you.
